Rules of Engagement & Ranking Protocol

RankUp operates an open, transparent, capital-backed discovery engine. Understand how real-time bids, floor increments, and cross-sector normalization determine rank position.

Strict Capital Precedence

Products are ranked strictly by verified current bid amount within their target category arena. If two listings share an identical bid amount, timestamp precedence governs: the earlier confirmed transaction retains rank advantage.

02
upgrade

Dynamic Floors & Bid Increments

Entering an arena for the first time requires meeting or exceeding the sector floor (minimum starting bid). Bumping an incumbent rank holder requires topping their active bid by at least the category's minimum increment (typically $1 to $5).

03
equalizer

Cross-Sector Score Normalization

The Global Live Leaderboard and Daily Feed implement an intelligent normalization factor across sectors. High-capital enterprise categories do not drown out exceptional indie developer tools or niche utilities.

Cryptographic Settlement & Finality

Every bid is processed and authorized through payment gateways (Razorpay) before being committed to the public ledger. Once payment is confirmed, ranks update instantaneously across all connected clients via WebSockets. Bids are final.

Verified Traffic & Analytics Tracking

Outbound clicks from RankUp to your product URL are tracked and filtered against automated bot scrapers in real-time. High-ranking spots enjoy verifiable exposure metrics visible directly on public listing profiles.

06

Continuous Reset & Daily Snapshots

Rankings are live and continuous 24/7/365. Daily snapshots of winning bids are archived at 00:00 UTC and immortalized into the Daily Listings history so top performers maintain permanent proof of dominance.

Frequently Asked Inquiries

What happens when my project is outbid?

You do not lose your listing! When another project places a higher bid, your product automatically shifts down to the next rank. You can reclaim your previous position at any time by placing a new bump bid.

Can I update my product's title, URL, or metadata?

Yes. Re-bidding on an existing listing automatically updates its metadata (scraped from your URL) while maintaining your verified click history and transaction record.

How do category arenas differ from the Global Leaderboard?

Category Arenas are dedicated vertical leaderboards (e.g. Developer Tools, AI & Agents) ranked purely by dollar bids. The Global Leaderboard features top contenders weighted across all sectors for maximum site-wide visibility.
