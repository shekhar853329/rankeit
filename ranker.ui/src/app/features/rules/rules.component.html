Highest Bid Takes Rank #1

Rankings are calculated directly by your active bid amount within your chosen category. The highest bid sits at Rank #1, the second highest at Rank #2, and so on.

schedule
Early-Bird Tiebreaker: If two products have the exact same bid amount, whichever product placed its initial bid first keeps the higher rank. Early adopters always retain their time advantage!
02
ads_click

Bid for #1 or Any Position

You don't have to bid for Rank #1 right away. You can place any bid amount starting from as low as ₹1.00 to enter the leaderboard.

flag
To claim Rank #1: Your bid must beat the current leader by at least the category increment (e.g. +₹1). If the category is brand new, simply match the category starting price.
03
savings

You Only Pay the Difference!

When you want to raise your rank or reclaim the top spot, you never pay the full amount again. Your existing active bid is credited 100% toward your new target.

check_circle
Example: If your current bid is ₹150 and you want to raise your bid to ₹220, you only pay ₹70 at checkout. Your new displayed bid becomes ₹220!

Today's Auction vs. All-Time Rank

RankUp provides two distinct views to maximize discoverability:

  • Today's Auction: Features products that placed or upgraded a bid today (UTC). This is the active daily feed that visitors check to discover what's hot right now.
  • All-Time Rankings: The permanent leaderboard showing every product ranked by its all-time highest active bid.
  • Hall of Fame: A prestigious showcase celebrating the platform-wide top bidders across all categories.
05
swap_vert

You Never Lose Your Listing

If another creator places a higher bid and overtakes your position, your product is never removed.

Your listing smoothly shifts down to the next position (e.g. from #1 to #2) and continues receiving visitors. You can reclaim your top spot at any time simply by topping up the difference.

Direct Traffic & Protected Ownership

To protect your listing, your domain is locked to your contact email. Only you can raise bids or edit details for your product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay every day to stay on the leaderboard?

No! Once placed, your bid is permanent on the All-Time Leaderboard for as long as your listing is active. To appear in Today's Auction, you simply need to have placed a bid or top-up on that day.

Can I lower my bid amount or withdraw it?

No. Bids can only be increased, never decreased. This ensures complete transparency, stability, and fairness for all competing products on the leaderboard.

Can someone else change or bid on my website URL?

No. Every listing is securely tied to the contact email you provided during your first bid. If someone tries to submit a bid on your website using a different email, the system rejects it immediately.

What if another bidder places a bid at the exact same moment?

Our system processes transactions securely in real time. If a competitor's bid confirms a fraction of a second before yours and renders your bid too low, your funds are never lost—they are automatically held safely for immediate reconciliation or refund.

Where do users go when they click on my listing?

Visitors are sent directly to your website URL in a new browser tab. We don't route them through annoying interstitial pages or ad walls.

Can I update my product's name, description, or logo?

Yes! Whenever you place a top-up bid on your existing listing, our system automatically re-scrapes your URL's updated metadata (title, logo, and description) to keep your profile fresh.
