Rules

How ranking and bidding works on RankIt.

  1. Listings are ranked strictly by current bid amount within their category; ties go to whoever bid first.
  2. A new bid must exceed the category's current top bid by at least the category's minimum increment.
  3. A brand-new listing must meet the category's minimum starting bid.
  4. The homepage and "Day wise listing" page use a normalized score across categories so high-price verticals don't automatically dominate.
  5. Bids are final once payment is confirmed; reclaiming a spot means placing a new, higher bid.
