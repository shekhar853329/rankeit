Top Placement Takes Rank #1

Rankings are calculated directly by your active sponsored placement amount within your chosen category. The highest placement sits at Rank #1, the second highest at Rank #2, and so on.

Early-Bird Tiebreaker: If two products have the exact same placement amount, whichever product placed its initial listing first keeps the higher rank. Early adopters always retain their time advantage!

You Only Pay the Difference!

When you want to raise your rank or reclaim the top spot, you never pay the full amount again. Your existing active placement is credited 100% toward your new target.

check_circle
Example: If your current placement is $150 and you want to raise your rank to $220, you only pay $70 at checkout. Your new displayed placement becomes $220!

Today vs. All-Time Rank

RankUp provides two distinct views to maximize discoverability:

  • Today: Features products that placed or upgraded a sponsored placement today (UTC). This is the active daily feed that visitors check to discover what's hot right now.
  • All-Time Rankings: The permanent leaderboard showing every product ranked by its all-time highest active placement.
  • Hall of Fame: A prestigious showcase celebrating the platform-wide top featured products across all categories.
05

You Never Lose Your Listing

If another creator places a higher promotional amount and overtakes your position, your product is never removed.

Your listing smoothly shifts down to the next position (e.g. from #1 to #2) and continues receiving visitors. You can reclaim your top spot at any time simply by topping up the difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay every day to stay on the leaderboard?

No! Once placed, your placement is permanent on the All-Time Leaderboard for as long as your listing is active. To appear in Today, you simply need to have placed a placement or top-up on that day.

Can I lower my placement amount or withdraw it?

No. Placements can only be increased, never decreased. This ensures complete transparency, stability, and fairness for all competing products on the leaderboard.

Can someone else change or promote my website URL?

No. Every listing is securely tied to the contact email you provided during your first placement. If someone tries to submit a placement on your website using a different email, the system rejects it immediately.

What if another creator places a placement at the exact same moment?

Our system processes transactions securely in real time. If a competitor's payment confirms a fraction of a second before yours and renders your placement too low, your funds are never lost—they are automatically held safely for immediate reconciliation or refund.

Where do users go when they click on my listing?

Visitors are sent directly to your website URL in a new browser tab. We don't route them through annoying interstitial pages or ad walls.

Can I update my product's name, description, or logo?

Yes! Whenever you place an upgrade on your existing listing, our system automatically re-scrapes your URL's updated metadata (title, logo, and description) to keep your profile fresh.
