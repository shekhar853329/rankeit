What We Collect

We keep the Service small. We collect only what we need to run the board, take payment, stop abuse, and understand traffic.

cookie Visitor Identifier

A random ID in an HTTP-only cookie (rankit-visitor-id), stored for up to one year. We use it to attach a checkout to a browser, limit duplicate click counts, and keep the board usable. It is not your name.

shopping_cart Checkout & Listing Data

The URL you submit, the category, rank amount, and the public name, description, and image we resolve for that destination. Payment confirmation identifiers (order and checkout IDs) are stored so we can place the ranking.

credit_card Payment Data

Card details and billing identity are collected by Payment Gateway, not by us. Payment Gateway may send us confirmation that you paid and an amount. See Payment Gateway's own privacy notice for details.

ads_click Clicks

When you visit a listing from the board, we may record the listing, a timestamp, the visitor ID, and a hashed IP address. The hash is for rate limiting and fake-click reduction, not to profile you.

dns Technical Data

Standard request data such as user agent, referrer, and IP address may be processed by our hosting infrastructure to operate and secure the Service.

mail Messages You Send Us

If you email a notice, a category correction, or a privacy request, we keep that correspondence as needed to respond and to maintain a legal record.

cookie

Cookies

The visitor-ID cookie is a strictly necessary cookie. It lets checkout and click protection work. We do not use it for advertising.

Your browser or device may also store a theme preference locally so the page can stay light or dark between visits. This is stored entirely on your device and not sent to our servers.

info
We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. Analytics, if any, uses cookieless methods that do not identify you personally.

Why We Use This Data

Contract

To take payment, create or raise a listing, show rank, and provide the Service you asked for.

Legitimate Interests

To keep the board fair (rate limits, bot filtering), display public listing metadata, measure visits, debug outages, and defend legal claims. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us.

Consent

To send any product digest or notification emails you explicitly asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing or emailing us.

Legal Obligation

To keep tax, accounting, and complaint records where the law requires it.

public

Public Listings

Rank, spend amounts, names, images, descriptions, and destination links on the board are public. Anyone can see them, including search engines.

warning
Do not list a destination if you do not want that information shown publicly. We fetch public metadata from the site you submit so visitors can recognize the listing. That fetch may disclose to the destination that RankUp requested the page.

  • Visitor IDs Up to one year in the cookie, or until you clear cookies.
  • Public Ranking Entries While the listing is on the board; may remain in backups for a limited time after a takedown.

  • Click Records & IP Hashes Only as long as useful for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and aggregate counts.
  • Correspondence As long as needed to respond and maintain a legal record.
